The Challenge

Diabetic foot ulcers are a leading cause of hospitalizations, but they don't happen overnight. Current methods rely on manual, infrequent checks, missing the critical window for prevention.

Late Detection

Ulcers often go unnoticed until it's too late due to neuropathy and loss of sensation.

Hospital Dependency

Frequent clinic visits are required, placing a heavy burden on patients and healthcare systems.

Manual Monitoring

Subjective daily self-checks are unreliable and often forgotten by patients.

Risk of Amputation

Every 20 seconds, a lower limb is lost to diabetes globally. It's time for a change.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Diab-Aid predict ulcers? +
Our AI models analyze continuous pressure, temperature, and humidity data to identify micro-traumas and inflammation patterns weeks before a physical ulcer forms.
Is my medical data secure? +
Yes, Diab-Aid uses Supabase Cloud with end-to-end encryption, ensuring full compliance with HIPAA and global data privacy standards.
Do I need to charge the Smart Shoe? +
Yes, the smart insoles have a long-lasting battery that typically requires wireless charging once a week, depending on usage.
Can my current doctor access the data? +
Absolutely. You can invite your podiatrist or primary care physician to the Doctor Workspace to securely review your real-time data.
